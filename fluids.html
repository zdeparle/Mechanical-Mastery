💧 Fluid Mechanics

Learn about pressure, flow, and fluid dynamics

Pressure & Hydrostatics

$P = \frac{F}{A}$
P - Pressure
Pressure (Pa, kPa, MPa, psi, atm)
F - Force
Force normal to surface (N, kN, lbf)
A - Area
Surface area (m², cm², in²)
$P = P_0 + \rho gh$
P - Pressure
Pressure at depth h (Pa, kPa, psi)
P₀ - Surface Pressure
Pressure at surface (Pa, kPa, psi)
ρ - Density
Fluid density (kg/m³, g/cm³, lb/ft³)
g - Gravitational Acceleration
Acceleration due to gravity (m/s², ft/s²)
h - Depth
Depth below surface (m, cm, ft, in)

Continuity & Flow

$Q = Av = \text{constant}$
Q - Volumetric Flow Rate
Volume flow rate (m³/s, L/s, ft³/s, gpm)
A - Cross-sectional Area
Pipe cross-sectional area (m², cm², in²)
v - Velocity
Flow velocity (m/s, cm/s, ft/s, mph)
$P + \frac{1}{2}\rho v^2 + \rho gh = \text{constant}$
P - Pressure
Static pressure (Pa, kPa, psi)
ρ - Density
Fluid density (kg/m³, g/cm³, lb/ft³)
v - Velocity
Flow velocity (m/s, ft/s)
g - Gravitational Acceleration
Acceleration due to gravity (m/s², ft/s²)
h - Height
Elevation above reference (m, ft)

Reynolds Number

$Re = \frac{\rho vD}{\mu}$
Re - Reynolds Number
Dimensionless flow parameter
ρ - Density
Fluid density (kg/m³, g/cm³, lb/ft³)
v - Velocity
Flow velocity (m/s, ft/s)
D - Characteristic Length
Pipe diameter or characteristic length (m, cm, in, ft)
μ - Dynamic Viscosity
Dynamic viscosity (Pa·s, cP, lb·s/ft²)
