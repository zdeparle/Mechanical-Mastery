📐 Machine Design

Design principles for mechanical components and systems

Factor of Safety

$n = \frac{\sigma_{allowable}}{\sigma_{actual}} = \frac{F_{yield}}{F_{applied}}$
n - Factor of Safety
Safety factor (dimensionless, typically 1.5-4)
σ_allowable - Allowable Stress
Maximum allowed stress (Pa, MPa, psi)
σ_actual - Actual Stress
Calculated stress in component (Pa, MPa, psi)
F_yield - Yield Force
Force at yield point (N, kN, lbf)
F_applied - Applied Force
Actual applied force (N, kN, lbf)

Beam Bending

$\sigma = \frac{Mc}{I}$
σ - Bending Stress
Maximum bending stress (Pa, MPa, psi)
M - Bending Moment
Bending moment (N·m, lb·ft)
c - Distance to Neutral Axis
Distance from neutral axis to outer fiber (m, cm, in)
I - Moment of Inertia
Area moment of inertia (m⁴, cm⁴, in⁴)
$S = \frac{I}{c}$
S - Section Modulus
Section modulus (m³, cm³, in³)
I - Moment of Inertia
Area moment of inertia (m⁴, cm⁴, in⁴)
c - Distance to Neutral Axis
Distance from neutral axis (m, cm, in)

Power Transmission

$P = T\omega = \frac{2\pi NT}{60}$
P - Power
Power transmitted (W, kW, hp)
T - Torque
Torque (N·m, lb·ft)
ω - Angular Velocity
Angular velocity (rad/s, rpm)
N - Rotational Speed
Rotational speed (rpm)
