📳 Vibrations

Analyze oscillatory motion and resonance in systems

Simple Harmonic Motion

$x(t) = A\sin(\omega t + \phi)$
x - Displacement
Position at time t (m, cm, in)
A - Amplitude
Maximum displacement (m, cm, in)
ω - Angular Frequency
Angular frequency (rad/s)
t - Time
Time (s, ms)
φ - Phase Angle
Initial phase (rad, deg)
$\omega_n = \sqrt{\frac{k}{m}}$
ω_n - Natural Frequency
Undamped natural frequency (rad/s, Hz)
k - Spring Constant
Spring stiffness (N/m, kN/m, lb/in)
m - Mass
Mass of oscillating system (kg, g, lb)

Damping

$\zeta = \frac{c}{2\sqrt{km}}$
ζ - Damping Ratio
Dimensionless damping parameter
c - Damping Coefficient
Viscous damping coefficient (N·s/m, lb·s/ft)
k - Spring Constant
Spring stiffness (N/m, lb/in)
m - Mass
Mass (kg, lb)

Resonance

$r = \frac{\omega}{\omega_n}$
r - Frequency Ratio
Ratio of forcing to natural frequency (dimensionless)
ω - Forcing Frequency
Frequency of external force (rad/s, Hz)
ω_n - Natural Frequency
Natural frequency of system (rad/s, Hz)
