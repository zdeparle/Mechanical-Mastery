🔩 Materials Science

Stress, strain, elastic behavior, and material properties for engineering design

Stress & Strain

$\sigma = \frac{F}{A}$
σ — Normal Stress
Force per unit area (Pa, MPa, psi)
F — Axial Force
Force along the member axis (N, kN, lbf)
A — Cross-sectional Area
Area perpendicular to force (m², mm²)
$\varepsilon = \frac{\Delta L}{L_0}$
ε — Normal Strain
Dimensionless deformation ratio
ΔL — Change in Length
Elongation or compression (m, mm)
L₀ — Original Length
Initial undeformed length (m, mm)

Hooke's Law & Elastic Behavior

$\sigma = E\varepsilon$
σ — Stress
Applied stress (Pa, MPa)
E — Young's Modulus
Material stiffness (Pa, GPa)
ε — Strain
Resulting strain (dimensionless)
$\nu = -\frac{\varepsilon_{lateral}}{\varepsilon_{axial}}$
ν — Poisson's Ratio
Ratio of lateral to axial strain (typically 0.25–0.35)
ε_lateral — Lateral Strain
Strain perpendicular to load (dimensionless)
ε_axial — Axial Strain
Strain along load direction (dimensionless)

Shear & Torsion

$\tau = \frac{V}{A}$
τ — Shear Stress
Average shear stress (Pa, MPa)
V — Shear Force
Force parallel to area (N, kN)
A — Shear Area
Area resisting shear (m², mm²)
$\tau = G\gamma$
τ — Shear Stress
Applied shear stress (Pa, MPa)
G — Shear Modulus
Modulus of rigidity (Pa, GPa)
γ — Shear Strain
Angular distortion (rad, dimensionless)

Design Criteria

$FOS = \frac{\sigma_{yield}}{\sigma_{working}}$
FOS — Factor of Safety
Margin of safety (dimensionless, typically > 1)
σ_yield — Yield Strength
Material yield stress (Pa, MPa)
σ_working — Working Stress
Actual applied stress (Pa, MPa)
$\varepsilon_T = \alpha \Delta T$
ε_T — Thermal Strain
Strain from temperature change (dimensionless)
α — Coefficient of Thermal Expansion
Material property (1/K, 1/°C)
ΔT — Temperature Change
Change in temperature (K, °C)
