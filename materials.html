Stress & Strain

$\sigma = \frac{F}{A}$
σ - Stress
Normal stress (Pa, kPa, MPa, GPa, psi, ksi)
F - Force
Applied force (N, kN, lbf)
A - Cross-sectional Area
Area perpendicular to force (m², cm², mm², in²)
$\epsilon = \frac{\Delta L}{L_0}$
ε - Strain
Normal strain (dimensionless)
ΔL - Change in Length
Change in length (m, cm, mm, in, ft)
L₀ - Original Length
Original length (m, cm, mm, in, ft)
$E = \frac{\sigma}{\epsilon}$
E - Young's Modulus
Elastic modulus (Pa, GPa, psi, ksi)
σ - Stress
Normal stress (Pa, GPa, psi)
ε - Strain
Normal strain (dimensionless)

Shear Stress & Strain

$\tau = \frac{V}{A}$
τ - Shear Stress
Shear stress (Pa, MPa, psi)
V - Shear Force
Applied shear force (N, kN, lbf)
A - Area
Area parallel to force (m², cm², in²)
$G = \frac{\tau}{\gamma}$
G - Shear Modulus
Modulus of rigidity (Pa, GPa, psi)
τ - Shear Stress
Shear stress (Pa, GPa, psi)
γ - Shear Strain
Shear strain (dimensionless, rad)

Poisson's Ratio

$\nu = -\frac{\epsilon_{lateral}}{\epsilon_{axial}}$
ν - Poisson's Ratio
Ratio of lateral to axial strain (dimensionless)
ε_lateral - Lateral Strain
Strain perpendicular to load (dimensionless)
ε_axial - Axial Strain
Strain parallel to load (dimensionless)
