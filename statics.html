Force Analysis

$F_x = F \cos(\theta)$, $F_y = F \sin(\theta)$
F - Force
Magnitude of the force vector (N, kN, lbf)
θ - Angle
Angle from horizontal axis (rad, deg)
F_x - Horizontal Component
Force component in x-direction (N, kN, lbf)
F_y - Vertical Component
Force component in y-direction (N, kN, lbf)
$F_R = \sqrt{F_x^2 + F_y^2}$
F_R - Resultant Force
Magnitude of resultant force (N, kN, lbf)
F_x - Horizontal Component
Sum of forces in x-direction (N, kN, lbf)
F_y - Vertical Component
Sum of forces in y-direction (N, kN, lbf)

Equilibrium

$T = mg$
T - Tension
Tension force in rope/cable (N, kN, lbf)
m - Mass
Mass of the object (kg, g, lb)
g - Gravitational Acceleration
Acceleration due to gravity (m/s², ft/s², g)

Moments & Torque

$M = F \times d$
M - Moment
Moment about a point (N·m, lb·ft)
F - Force
Applied force (N, kN, lbf)
d - Perpendicular Distance
Distance from force line to pivot (m, cm, ft, in)

Newton's Laws & Dynamics

$F = ma$
F - Net Force
Sum of all forces acting on object (N, kN, lbf)
m - Mass
Mass of the object (kg, g, lb)
a - Acceleration
Acceleration of the object (m/s², ft/s²)

Friction

$f_k = \mu_k N$
f_k - Kinetic Friction Force
Kinetic friction force (N, kN, lbf)
μ_k - Coefficient of Kinetic Friction
Dimensionless coefficient
N - Normal Force
Force perpendicular to surface (N, kN, lbf)
